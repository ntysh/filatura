Cycle counters

STOLL Operating Instructions
STOLL Operating Instructions, sections 3.1.2 and 4.1.9–4.1.11, manual pages 3-6 / 4-23–4-26

The “Cycle counters & counters” window is used to set the piece number or, for yard goods, the maximum number of revolutions with the MT counter.

Cycle counters RS1 to RS19 and other counters can also be adjusted. Other counters may be used by the knitting program to request conditions or to indicate machine states.
