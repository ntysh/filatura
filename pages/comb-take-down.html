Comb take-down

STOLL Operating Instructions
STOLL Operating Instructions, section 2.8.3, manual pages 2-33–2-34

The take-down comb automatically starts fabric pieces and presses them off after completion.

At the beginning of a panel it grips the comb thread, moves the fabric downward and later transfers the fabric to the main take-down. Short shaped parts such as collars or trimmings can be taken down by the comb without the main or auxiliary take-down.
