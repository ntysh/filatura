Saves

[by tysh]
[memory note summer 2026]

In a computer game, saves are where your previous experiences are recorded. In a way, the most human part of the experience with the knitting machine is located here, with the «Read in/store data» function. The programs are pure intentions of how it should work. Here I spent the most of machine time, often picking between the versions of the programs to try to run them with different jacquard motifs. I would tap that icon of the floppy disk and then in the new menu scroll through the files of .jac, .sin, and .set extensions. I feel the most confident there. The repeated mistake was sometimes forgetting to load the file from the flash drive to the machine’s memory, as this file browsing navigation is only for lurking around. To read in/store we are tapping the new icon with a little T-shirt appearing from the abstract rhombus.
