STOLL Operating Instructions, documented Service-menu uses throughout chapters 3, 4 and 6

The manual does not give one compact definition of the Service menu. Its documented uses show it as a gateway to service, statistics, diagnostics and maintenance-related functions.

Examples include touch-screen calibration, report and shift-counter access, running-time monitoring and measurement, copying service data, and service-level manual interventions used during repair.
