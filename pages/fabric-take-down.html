Fabric take-down

STOLL Operating Instructions
STOLL Operating Instructions, section 2.8, manual pages 2-30–2-34

The fabric take-down consists of three units: the main take-down, the auxiliary take-down and the take-down comb. Each unit is driven by a separate motor and can be adapted to the knitting situation.

The main take-down pulls the stitches downward and guides the finished fabric into the collection chamber. The auxiliary take-down grasps the fabric directly under the needle bed, while the take-down comb automatically starts fabric pieces and presses them off after completion.
